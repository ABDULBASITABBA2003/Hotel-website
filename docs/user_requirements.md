# User Requirements

This document outlines the functional requirements for the Hotel-website, categorized by user role.

## Guest
The Guest is a visitor to the website looking for accommodation and information.

- The Guest shall be able to search for apartments for their vacation through the search bar.
- The Guest shall be able to view "Why choose us" information to understand the service's value propositions.
- The Guest shall be able to view special offers including accommodation details, dates, and pricing.
- The Guest shall be able to read customer reviews to assess the quality of the service and accommodations.
- The Guest shall be able to read recent blog posts to get travel tips and information.
- The Guest shall be able to subscribe to the newsletter by providing their email address.
- The Guest shall be able to toggle the navigation menu on mobile devices.
- The Guest shall be able to navigate to sections like "Last Minute Deals", "Blog", "About us", and "Contacts".
- The Guest shall be able to access the service's social media profiles (Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, Telegram).
- The Guest shall be able to find contact information, such as the phone number provided in the support section.
- The Guest shall be able to view options for changing currency and language (indicated by icons in the header).
